# TAPPaaS Documentation

**TAPPaaS** — *Trusted, Automated, Private Platform as a Service* — is an open-source,
self-hosted platform for **digital sovereignty**, built on Proxmox. It packages the four
building blocks of a real cloud — site, workloads, people and environments — pre-integrated
and automated, on hardware you own.

Your data. Your costs. Your access. **Your platform.**
