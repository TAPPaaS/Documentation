    Sovereignty is architecture,
    not a contract.

    Every cloud sits somewhere on a continuum. On the far side of the glass wall your digital life runs on someone else’s terms — their datacenter, their prices, their access policies. TAPPaaS moves you to your side of the wall: the same convenience, but on your architecture. And because it is architecture, it is resilient — your platform keeps running even when the internet doesn’t.

    A cloud is four building blocks.
    Now they’re yours.

    Hyperscalers won by packaging complexity: a site to run on, workloads to run, people to serve, and environments to keep them apart. TAPPaaS packages the same four blocks — pre-integrated and open source — on your side of the wall.

    01

    Site

    Proxmox cluster, storage, network and firewall — the ground your cloud stands on.

    02

    Workloads

    Apps and services installed, updated and backed up by the platform — not by hand.

    03

    People

    Identity, single sign-on and access control for households, teams and tenants.

    04

    Environments

    Clean separation for production, family, tenants and experiments — blast doors included.

    Trusted. Automated. Private.

    T

    Trusted

    Open source end to end (MPL 2.0). Every component inspectable, every claim checkable. No black boxes between you and your data.

    A

    Automated

    One CICD mothership installs, updates, monitors and heals the whole platform. Enterprise operations without an ops team.

    P

    Private

    Self-hosted by design. Your data never has to leave your building — and your AI can run entirely on your own silicon.
